# Joint Graph

First create a crank-rocker mechanism as in [Making Your First Mechanism](./making-your-first-mechanism/understanding-mechanism-design#draw-a-four-bar-mechanism). Then continue as below.

Open the _Joint Graph_ panel and select the coupler point of interest. The joint graph highlights the selected joint and shows the displacement, velocity, and acceleration of that joint in linear units.
